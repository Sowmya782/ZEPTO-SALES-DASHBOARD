# SALES AND DELIVERY DASHBOARD (Interactive Dashboard using MS Excel)

---

## PROJECT OBJECTIVES

- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Monitor key business KPIs.
- Analyze customer and product performance.
- Compare sales across different cities and customer segments.
- Analyze sales based on payment methods.
- Monitor delivery status and delivery performance.
- Identify top-performing products.
- Support business decision-making through data visualization.

## Questions (KPIs)

- What is the average sales per order?

- Which payment method is most commonly used?
- Which products are the top performers?
- What is the overall delivery status of orders?

## Project Insights

- The dashboard analyzes sales, orders, customers, products, and delivery performance.
- Total sales generated are approximately 562,992.
- A total of 1,500 orders were recorded.
- The dataset contains 300 customers and 200 products.
- The total quantity sold is 4,207.
- Bangalore generates the highest sales among the cities.
- Bulk customers contribute the highest share of sales.
- Office is the highest-performing customer segment by sales.
- UPI is one of the major payment methods used by customers.
- Most orders have a Delivered status.
- Sales performance can be compared across months, cities, customer types, and payment methods.
